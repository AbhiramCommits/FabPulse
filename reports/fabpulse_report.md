## Glossary

- **UCL / LCL** — Upper and lower control limits, drawn at the process mean ± 3 standard deviations; a point outside them is unlikely under stable random variation.
- **MTBF** — Mean time between failures: productive hours divided by the number of unscheduled-down episodes.
- **MTTR** — Mean time to repair: total unscheduled-down hours divided by the number of unscheduled-down episodes.
- **IQR** — Interquartile range, the distance between the 25th and 75th percentiles; Tukey outliers lie beyond 1.5×IQR from the box.
- **p-value** — The probability of seeing a difference at least this large if nothing real changed; below 0.05 is conventionally called significant.
- **Availability (OEE)** — Productive time divided by planned production time, excluding scheduled maintenance but including standby, engineering and unplanned downtime.
- **Rolling mean** — The average of a metric over a trailing window of days, used to separate signal from day-to-day noise.
